What makes me different?

I believe in writing learning content that is completely relatable. By tapping into the "If I wouldn't do it, I wouldn't ask my learners to do it" mindset, I focus on crafting real-world scenarios, interactive components, and thought-provoking media that bypass standard, uninspiring template check-boxes.

Instead of building massive, months-long courses, I specialize in jumping onto your short-term contracts to deliver high-quality, high-impact assets on quick timelines.

Visual Design

With a background UX/UI and Graphic Design, I have years of real-world experience. When crafting learning experiences I intentionally use design elements like layout, typography, and color to deliver content in a way that makes sense for your learners. I specialize in developing interactive graphics and custom imagery to take your learning from mid, to magnificent!

Learning Experience Design

I am a licensed professional with a Master of Arts in Education and sound knowledge of adult learning theory, multimedia principles and instructional design models. My experience has taught me to create accessible, interactive learning that drives learner engagement and retention.

Digital Learning Development

Bringing curriculum to life brings me satisfaction! I can flex my skillset to meet your needs, so whether you need interactive web graphics, a quick prototype for a new course module, or a professional edit of raw footage, I got your back! It is my aim to create equitable courses that are exciting while keeping accessibility at the foundation.
