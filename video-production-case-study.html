Documentary-Style Educational Video Production

Elevating Educational Video Through Collaborative Fieldwork

Overview

Beyond standard technical tutorials and static course media, our Learning Production (LP) team at RMCAD produces immersive, documentary-style educational video assets that bring academic theory into real-world practice. When designing an advanced course on 21st-century museum leadership, we partnered directly with executive directors at two Denver cultural institutions. By executing on-location shoots and refining our post-production design between site visits, we created modular video assets that measurably enhanced learner engagement and topic retention.

Discovery & Pedagogical Alignment

Rather than relying strictly on static textbook readings and instructor lecture recordings, our ICDs and LXDs collaborated closely with the Subject Matter Expert (SME) to align interview prompts directly with Course Learning Outcomes (CLOs), grounding abstract leadership concepts in authentic, executive-level dialogue.

Institution Focus 01

Museo de las Americas

Centered on community-driven leadership, Latinx and Indigenous heritage, civic responsibility, and addressing the immediate social needs of diverse urban communities.

Institution Focus 02

The Clyfford Still Museum

Centered on single-artist legacy preservation, collections care, and balancing artist intent with modern audience activation and democratic access.

Fieldwork Operations & Team Coordination

Executing field shoots in active museum spaces required dynamic cross-functional coordination between ICDs, LXDs, course SMEs, and museum leadership.

Standardized Shoot Window

Precise 2-hour shoot structure—30 minutes for setup and breakdown, 30 minutes for executive interviews, and 60 minutes to capture gallery and behind-the-scenes B-roll. We can work with your team to schedule a shoot that works for you.

Collaborative Team Roles

ICDs and LXDs share responsibilities on set—managing technical setup and guiding interviews with targeted follow-up questions informed by deep course knowledge.

Targeted B-Roll Shot Lists

Highly relevant B-roll collection directly reinforces the spoken narrative and connects concrete imagery with abstract concepts.

Responsive Growth Between Shoots

Our team values continuous improvement and shares draft review links with participants post-shoot to gather feedback prior to finalizing the edit because we know that real growth happens through continuous iteration.

Shoot 01 Museo de las Americas

Continuous Narrative Edit

Produced a seamless, uninterrupted interview edit without visual editorial prompts or structural breaks.

Post-Shoot Insight

While the interview content was rich, learners struggled to identify thematic transitions between distinct concepts like community engagement and financial management without visual cues.

Shoot 02 Clyfford Still Museum

Visible Editorial Signposts

Introduced explicit on-screen question callouts displaying exact course module prompts, paired with archival materials and rhythmic B-roll cutaways.

Applied Solution & Outcome

Visual signposts gave students clear mental breaks, improving retention and allowing them to track topic transitions effortlessly.
