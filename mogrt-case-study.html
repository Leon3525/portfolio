Motion Graphic Templates for Scalable Video Systems

Standardizing Brand-Driven Video Assets for Rapid Post-Production

Overview

Motion graphic templates are an essential part of any scalable video production system. Moving beyond simply creating one off graphics a standardized template system enables efficient video production with consistent branding. By developing a reusable title bumper template in Adobe after effects, I streamlined the RMCAD video production process. This work was essential to speed up video production process for the learning production department.

The Core Problem & Target Audience

Organizations struggle with efficient and consistent production of video assets.

This case study is for enterprise organizations seeking to unify and speed up their video production process.

Project Scope & Constraints

I was tasked with developing a reusable asset for video bumpers for course content, instructional videos at Rocky Mountain College and design.

I was constrained to only video bumpers so this work did not include any kind of chapter titles or interview question cards or video overlays.

Research & Needs Analysis

I referenced legacy assets and conducted a thorough needs analysis. Identifying the need for a streamline system that includes bumpers for videos created for the new interdisciplinary studies department.

As I researched, I discovered a need to unify all departments within the institution while also highlighting specific departments as they pertain two videos.

Analysis Point 01

Institutional Unification

Establishing a single visual foundation that reinforces overarching institutional brand integrity across all published content.

Analysis Point 02

Department Flexibility

Providing modular controls for editors to highlight specific academic departments (e.g., Interdisciplinary Studies) without breaking core styling rules.
